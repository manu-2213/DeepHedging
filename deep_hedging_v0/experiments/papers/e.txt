\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Data and Calibration}

Parameters are calibrated annually to S\&P~500 index options for the years 2017--2021 using the QuantLib implementation of the Heston model and log-normal implied volatility for BSM.  Each calibration yields one spot level $S_0$, one set of five near-the-money strikes $K \in \mathbb{R}^5$ (spaced at approximately one index point around $S_0$), and the corresponding model parameters ($\sigma$ for BSM; $\kappa, \theta, \rho, \xi, v_0$ for Heston).

The temporal split is deterministic: years 2017--2020 form the training set (4 annual configurations); year 2021 is the held-out test set (1 configuration).  No data leakage is possible since calibrated parameters are fixed constants in the simulator—they are never updated during training.

\subsection{Experimental Design}

Experiments span a $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 2$ factorial design:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Architecture:} baseline single-policy $\times$ Actor--Inactor.
	\item \textbf{Network type:} MLP $\times$ LSTM (history window $L=5$).
\item \textbf{Option type:} European call / down-and-out call / cash-or-nothing digital.
\item \textbf{Dynamics:} BSM / Heston.
\end{itemize}
This yields 24 configurations.  Each is trained under 10 independent seeds (PPO stochasticity only; simulation paths are fixed within a seed via the vectorised environment's internal RNG).  The total sweep is $240$ independent runs.

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}

After training, each model is evaluated on the test environment (2021 calibration, separate from training).  A single deterministic rollout is performed (exploration disabled) and the terminal P\&L for each of the $500$ (BSM) or $100$ (Heston) collapsed paths is recorded:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{PnL}_i = V_N^{(i)} - C_N^{(i)}, \quad i = 1,\ldots,M.
\end{equation}

From the sample $\{\mathrm{PnL}_i\}_{i=1}^M$ the following metrics are computed.

\paragraph{Central tendency and dispersion.}
Mean $\bar\mu = M^{-1}\sum_i \mathrm{PnL}_i$ and standard deviation $\hat\sigma$.

\paragraph{Risk-adjusted returns.}
Sharpe ratio $\hat\mu/\hat\sigma$ and Sortino ratio $\hat\mu/\hat\sigma_-$, where
$\hat\sigma_- = \sqrt{M_-^{-1}\sum_{i:\,\mathrm{PnL}_i<0} \mathrm{PnL}_i^{\,2}}$
is the root-mean-square loss over the $M_-$ negative paths.

\paragraph{Tail risk.}
Value-at-Risk and Conditional VaR (Expected Shortfall) at $\alpha \in \{0.95, 0.99\}$,
using the loss convention $L_i = -\mathrm{PnL}_i$:
\begin{align}
\widehat{\mathrm{VaR}}_\alpha &= \mathrm{Quantile}_{\alpha}(\{L_i\}), \\
\widehat{\mathrm{CVaR}}_\alpha &= \mathbb{E}\!\left[L \mid L \ge \widehat{\mathrm{VaR}}_\alpha\right].
\end{align}

\paragraph{Higher moments.}
Skewness and excess kurtosis of $\{\mathrm{PnL}_i\}$.

\paragraph{Aggregation across seeds.}
Each metric is first computed per seed, then averaged over the 10 seeds.  Confidence intervals are bootstrapped over seeds.  Architecture comparisons use paired $t$-tests across seeds to control for shared simulation conditions.

\subsection{Training Diagnostics}

Training progress is logged to Weights \& Biases at the episode level.  Reported per-episode statistics include total PPO loss, critic loss, policy loss, entropy loss, and mean episodic reward $\bar r = F^{-1}\sum_{t,i} r_t^{(i)}$.  The RL reward $\bar r$ is a step-level tracking-error average and serves as a diagnostic separate from the terminal P\&L metrics used for the main evaluation.
